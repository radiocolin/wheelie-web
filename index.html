Track every ride

Wheelie follows your ride live on the map, keeps every route you’ve ridden, and makes the good ones easy to share. Made for iPhone, and redesigned for iOS 27.

Ten rides free, then unlimited with a subscription.

On the ride

Your ride, live on the map

Tap Start and go. Your route draws itself on a full-screen map as you ride, with your speed, heading, distance, climb and time right below. Pull the card up for big, bright numbers you can read at a glance.

After the ride

Relive every route

Every ride keeps its full route, colored by how fast you were going, along with where you started and finished, the weather, and your notes.

  • Speed and elevation charts, minute by minute
  • Touch and hold the route to see your speed at any point
  • Distance, climb, average speed and time
History

Every ride, all in one place

See how far you’ve ridden this week, this month, or ever, and find any ride in seconds by date, place, distance, or tag.

  • Tag rides your way: commutes, long rides, weekends, trips
  • Pin the rides you want to keep at the top
  • Synced privately across your devices with iCloud
Sharing

Share the rides worth bragging about

Turn any ride into an image of its route over the map, with the distance, the place and the day. Choose which details to show, then send it or save it to Photos.

Anywhere

Wherever the road takes you

A loop around the park, a weekend on a canal trail, or the long way home through a new city: every ride comes home with you, wherever you ride it.

Beyond the app

Your ride, all over your iPhone

Keep riding without opening Wheelie. Your ride follows you to the Lock Screen, and your rides live on your Home Screen.

  • Lock Screen

    Speed, distance, climb and time in a Live Activity, with pause and stop right there.

  • Control Center

    Start, pause, and stop a ride from Control Center, without unlocking.

  • Home Screen

    Widgets for your latest ride, your recent rides, and your miles day by day.

  • Siri and Shortcuts

    Start a ride with your voice, or ask how far you’ve ridden this week.

And more

Everything a ride deserves

  • Apple Health

    Save rides to Health as cycling workouts with routes and calories, and bring in rides you recorded on your Apple Watch.

  • Take your rides anywhere

    Export a ride as GPX for Strava, Komoot, Garmin and other cycling apps, KML for maps, or JSON.

  • Weather on every ride

    Each ride remembers the temperature, wind, and conditions you rode in.

  • Tags and notes

    Tag rides with colors of your choice and jot down what made the ride worth remembering.

  • Smart, battery-friendly GPS

    Wheelie notices when you stop and start, so tracking stays precise without wasting battery.

  • Built for everyone

    VoiceOver, Voice Control, larger text, Reduce Motion, and high-contrast colors.

  • Your language, your units

    In English, Spanish, French, Dutch, and Danish, in miles or kilometers.

  • Light and dark

    Follows your iPhone’s appearance, or pick the one you like.

  • Landscape, too

    Mount your iPhone sideways and the ride view lays itself out for it.

Your rides stay yours

Your rides live on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account. Wheelie has no accounts and no servers of its own collecting where you ride.
